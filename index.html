Strict Governance

Everything the Application Follows

Dr.AI adheres strictly to leading healthcare, medical AI, legal, and privacy frameworks.

1. WHO ICD-11 Clinical Alignment

All AI diagnostic reasoning models, symptom classifiers, and differential diagnostic tables are mapped directly against the World Health Organization International Classification of Diseases (ICD-11) standard for global disease taxonomy.

Standard: WHO ICD-11 & PubMed Clinical Evidence

2. HIPAA Safe Harbor & Encryption

Patient Health Information (PHI) is protected via strict HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identification rules, zero unauthorized storage, and 256-Bit AES encryption for all WebRTC video streams, audio data, and clinical messages in transit and at rest.

Standard: HIPAA 45 CFR Part 160 & 164 (Security Rule)

3. FDA Digital Health CDS Guidance

Follows FDA Clinical Decision Support (CDS) non-device software guidance: The AI serves as an advisory triage and educational support system, providing transparent differential options and always prompting physician verification for medical intervention.

Standard: FDA Clinical Decision Support Software Guidance

4. Red-Flag Emergency Escalation

Automatic real-time keyword & semantic sentiment scanning for acute life-threatening cardiopulmonary and neurovascular symptoms (e.g. crushing chest pain, stroke signs, severe dyspnea) immediately triggers the high-priority Emergency 911 Dispatch Modal.

Protocol: Automatic Emergency Dispatch Trigger (911/112)

5. AMA Verified Physician Credentialing

Every consulting doctor on the network is verified through primary source medical board credentialing, active state medical licensure, malpractice insurance clearance, and adherence to American Medical Association (AMA) Telehealth Ethics.

Standard: AMA Code of Medical Ethics 1.2.12

6. Electronic Prescription (e-Rx) Rules

Digital prescription generation enforces strict structured fields (Medication name, generic compound, dosage, frequency schedule, duration, physician license signature stamp, and pharmacy allergy contraindication cross-checks).

Standard: DEA / Surescripts e-Prescribing Standard
